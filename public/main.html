THE CIRCULAR SYSTEM

One resource. Many outcomes.

BioCycle connects organic waste collection, biological conversion, renewable energy and resource recovery into one continuous loop.

🌾

01 — Feedstock

Organic material enters the system as cattle dung, food waste or agricultural biomass.

🦠

02 — Digestion

Microorganisms break down the biomass inside an oxygen-free digester.

🔥

03 — Biogas

The process produces a methane-rich biogas mixture that can be collected and used.

⚡

04 — Energy

Biogas can support applications such as cooking, heating, electricity generation or upgraded fuel systems.

🌱

05 — Digestate

The remaining digestate contains organic material and nutrients that can potentially return to agricultural systems.

🔄

06 — Resource Cycle

Resources are redirected back into useful applications instead of following a one-way waste pathway.
