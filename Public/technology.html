THE BIOLOGICAL PROCESS

Four stages. One transformation.

The actual biological system is complex, but the demonstration can be understood through four simplified stages.

01

Feedstock

Suitable biodegradable material enters the digester.

02

Hydrolysis

Complex organic compounds are broken into simpler forms.

03

Methanogenesis

Microorganisms produce methane-rich biogas.

04

Recovery

Biogas and digestate become system outputs.

THE DIGESTER

A controlled biological environment.

A digester provides an enclosed environment where organic material can undergo anaerobic biological decomposition.


Temperature, retention time, feedstock composition, microbial activity and system design can all influence the resulting gas production.

OUTPUTS

The process produces more than gas.
